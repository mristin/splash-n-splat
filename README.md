# Splash 'n' Splat

**Splash 'n' Splat is a snowball shooting gallery for children which they play with their hands in front of a webcam.**

Cheerful animals (polar bears, walruses, reindeer, foxes and many more) pop up from behind snowdrifts, igloos, rocks and trees.
Hold up an open hand to aim (a snowball outline follows it), and make a fist to throw.
Hit an animal before it winds up (watch the "!" bubble), or it throws a snowball back at you.
The animals throw at your head (you see a soft outline around it, which turns orange when a snowball comes): duck or step aside, and the snowball misses you.
You can also knock incoming snowballs out of the air; the ones which hit you splat on your screen, and the snow piles up around the edges.
Hit 20 animals to win a world; every snowball you take costs energy (the bar at the top right), and after ten you are snowed in.
The more energy you have left, the more stars you get.
If a world was too easy for you, more animals come out at once in the next one.

Cheeky penguins in bobble hats waddle along the snow in the front, and snowy owls flap across the sky; they throw at you while they move, and hitting them gives bonus points.
Kittens with big eyes stroll by, too: do not hit them! A hit kitten cries, and it costs you energy.
Balloons float by with boosters: hit one to get rapid fire (keep your fist closed and you throw again and again), big snowballs (easier to hit with) or energy.
The rapid fire and the big snowballs last only fifteen seconds.

A little plane takes you from Zurich to the North Pole on an old map of the Arctic, and then on to Greenland: walruses in bobble hats and spotted seals hide behind ice floes, icebergs and rocks in a fjord, and now and then an orca leaps slowly out of the sea; hit it for bonus points.

From there, the journey goes through eleven winter countries to Astana, each with its national animals in front of its landmark, and then back home to Zurich:

| Country | Animals | Landmark |
|---|---|---|
| Iceland | Arctic foxes, gyrfalcons, puffins | Hallgrímskirkja, a geyser |
| Norway | Reindeer, fjord horses | A stave church in a fjord |
| Sweden | Moose, blackbirds | Red cottages, a Dala horse |
| Finland | Brown bears, whooper swans | The northern lights, Helsinki Cathedral, a sauna |
| Estonia | Grey wolves, barn swallows | The towers of Tallinn |
| Latvia | White wagtails, two-spot ladybirds | The House of the Blackheads, the Freedom Monument |
| Lithuania | White storks, the Iron Wolf of the Vilnius legend | Gediminas' Tower |
| Belarus | European bison, beavers | Mir Castle |
| Ukraine | Nightingales, steppe marmots | St. Michael's Golden-Domed Monastery in Kyiv |
| Russia | Amur tigers, double-headed eagles | St. Basil's Cathedral, a Kremlin tower |
| Kazakhstan | Snow leopards, golden eagles, steppe horses | Bayterek, Khan Shatyr, yurts |
| Zurich | Red foxes | The Grossmünster, the lake and the Alps |

Nothing in it is violent: a hit animal only gets dizzy, giggles and ducks away.

Play with one hand or both: every hand gets its own snowball.
Friends can join any time, just by stepping in front of the camera.
You see yourselves dimly over the snowy world, like in a mirror, so you always know where your hands are.

**No network access.**
The game runs entirely on your computer and works offline.
The webcam images are processed locally, in memory, and are never saved or sent anywhere.
